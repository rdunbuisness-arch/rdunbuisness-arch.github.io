Deficit · pixel mockups

Workshop mockups for the Deficit calorie tracker. All numbers are example data.

  • A: Cozy 8-bit RPG HUD. Most game-like and atmospheric, but the dark theme and Press Start 2P numbers are slower to read at a glance.
  • B: Bright Arcade / Game Boy. Iconic and high-contrast, but the 4-shade green palette can't color deficit vs. surplus clearly.
  • C: Soft Pastel Pixel RECOMMENDED. Most readable and colorful, though less "gamey" than A.
  • D: Harvest Farm. Warm cozy-sim look; the harvest metaphor takes a moment to decode.
  • E: Candy Sticker Pop. Most playful and colorful; the tilts and stripes add some visual noise.
  • F: Fitness Quest. Motivating pixel-hero RPG look; the hero card takes up space.
